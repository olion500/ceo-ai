# Startup Validator Framework

## Core Question

**실제 비즈니스가 있는가?** (Is there a real business here?)

## Score: Market Opportunity (0-10)

This framework assesses whether the idea addresses a genuine market need with viable business potential.

## Evaluation Dimensions

### 1. Demand Signal Strength (0-10)

**What to assess:** Evidence that people actively want this solution.

| Score | Signal Level | Evidence |
|-------|-------------|---------|
| 0-2 | No signal | No search volume, no complaints, no workarounds |
| 3-4 | Weak signal | Some forum posts, low search volume |
| 5-6 | Moderate signal | Active communities discussing problem, some paid solutions |
| 7-8 | Strong signal | High search volume, multiple paid alternatives, vocal demand |
| 9-10 | Urgent signal | People asking to pay, pre-orders, waitlists exist |

**Evidence collection:**
- Search volume for problem-related keywords
- Forum/community discussion frequency
- Existing paid alternatives and their user counts
- Direct user interviews or survey results
- Social media mentions and complaint patterns

### 2. Market Size & Accessibility (0-10)

**What to assess:** How large is the addressable market and can you reach it?

| Score | Market Level | Criteria |
|-------|-------------|---------|
| 0-2 | Tiny/unreachable | <1K potential customers, no clear channels |
| 3-4 | Small/niche | 1K-10K customers, limited channels |
| 5-6 | Medium/accessible | 10K-100K customers, known channels |
| 7-8 | Large/reachable | 100K-1M customers, proven channels |
| 9-10 | Massive/easy reach | >1M customers, multiple organic channels |

**Key calculations:**
- TAM (Total Addressable Market)
- SAM (Serviceable Addressable Market)
- SOM (Serviceable Obtainable Market) — Year 1 realistic target

### 3. Pricing Power (0-10)

**What to assess:** Can you charge enough to build a sustainable business?

| Score | Pricing Level | Criteria |
|-------|-------------|---------|
| 0-2 | Race to bottom | Commodity product, price-sensitive buyers |
| 3-4 | Low pricing | <$10/mo, hard to differentiate on value |
| 5-6 | Moderate pricing | $10-50/mo, clear value proposition |
| 7-8 | Strong pricing | $50-200/mo, premium positioning possible |
| 9-10 | Premium pricing | >$200/mo or high one-time, customers see clear ROI |

**Pricing validation methods:**
- Competitor pricing benchmarks
- Willingness-to-pay signals (Van Westendorp, direct ask)
- Value-based pricing calculation (what's the problem worth?)
- Similar product revenue per user

### 4. Timing & Trend Alignment (0-10)

**What to assess:** Is the market timing favorable?

| Score | Timing Level | Criteria |
|-------|-------------|---------|
| 0-2 | Bad timing | Declining market, tech shift away |
| 3-4 | Neutral | Stable market, no particular urgency |
| 5-6 | Decent timing | Growing market, moderate tailwinds |
| 7-8 | Good timing | Strong growth trend, enabling tech maturing |
| 9-10 | Perfect timing | Explosive growth, new platform/regulation creating demand |

**Timing signals:**
- Google Trends direction
- Industry growth rate
- Technology enablers emerging
- Regulatory changes creating opportunity
- Platform shifts (AI, mobile, etc.)

## Scoring Formula

```
Market Opportunity = (Demand × 30%) + (Market Size × 25%) + (Pricing × 25%) + (Timing × 20%)
```

## Common Pitfalls

- **Confusing TAM with SOM**: Your Year 1 market is much smaller than total market
- **Assuming demand from your own need**: Personal pain ≠ market demand (validate!)
- **Ignoring pricing reality**: Market exists but won't pay enough for sustainability
- **Timing optimism**: "AI is hot" doesn't mean your specific AI tool has demand
