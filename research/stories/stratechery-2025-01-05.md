# Stratechery Success Story

## The Story

### Problem Discovery

Ben Thompson identified a gap in tech journalism: most coverage focused on **news and product announcements** rather than **strategic analysis**. While working at Microsoft, Apple, and Automattic, he observed that:

- Tech executives needed strategic insights, not just news
- Most tech bloggers covered "what" but not "why"
- No one was doing deep analysis of tech business strategy accessible to individuals

> "Anyone can come up with one really good post. It's a very distinct skill to come up with interesting things consistently."

### MVP Development

**Timeline:** 2013 (launched with backlog of content)

**Initial Features:**

- Blog posts analyzing tech strategy
- Free articles accessible to all
- Email newsletter for subscribers

**Tech Stack:**

- WordPress (initial)
- Custom theme with distinctive orange branding
- Hand-drawn iPad illustrations
- Email newsletter system

**Development Process:**

- Built content backlog before announcing site
- Focused on "consistency and regularity"
- Solo operation with laptop only

### Launch Strategy

**Platform:** Personal blog + Email newsletter

**Date:** 2013

**Pre-launch:**

- Wrote multiple articles to build backlog
- Cold emailed John Gruber of Daring Fireball

**Launch Results:**

- John Gruber discovered and endorsed Stratechery
- Twitter followers tripled in 12 hours (500 → 1,500)
- Initial traction established through tech influencer endorsement

### Growth Journey

#### Phase 1: Side Project (2013-2014)

- Wrote while employed at Automattic
- Built audience through consistent publishing
- Established reputation for strategic analysis

#### Phase 2: Failed Product Launch (April 2014)

- Left Automattic to go full-time
- Launched complex paid product with multiple subscription tiers
- Launch failed due to poor UX and broken security certificate
- **Critical pivot**: Started emailing daily updates instead

#### Phase 3: Finding Product-Market Fit (July-November 2014)

- Steady month-over-month growth
- 1,000 subscribers by November 2014
- ~$100,000 annual run rate ($100/subscriber/year)
- Announced 1,000 subscriber milestone → gained 250 new subscribers in 24 hours

#### Phase 4: Mature Growth (2015-Present)

- Steady linear growth (not exponential)
- Added podcast feeds, RSS, SMS options
- Launched Dithering podcast with John Gruber (2020)
- Added Sharp Tech and Sharp China podcasts
- Current: "tens of thousands" paid subscribers, millions free visitors

### Key Decisions

1. **Daily content discipline**
   - **Why:** Build subscriber confidence in long-term viability
   - **Result:** 2-3 articles weekly + interviews maintained consistently

2. **No breaking news**
   - **Why:** Focus on strategic analysis of public information
   - **Result:** Positioned as independent analyst, not journalist

3. **Paywall design**
   - **Why:** Avoid "walled garden" psychology that hurt Andrew Sullivan
   - **Result:** Free articles visible, subscription feels like additional value

4. **Email as primary distribution**
   - **Why:** Accidental discovery that email worked better than web-only
   - **Result:** Higher engagement and retention

5. **Bundling strategy (2020+)**
   - **Why:** Increase perceived value without raising prices
   - **Result:** Added podcasts to base subscription

### Pivots & Changes

- **Failed web portal → Email newsletter:** Completely changed distribution after initial launch failed
- **Experimented with sponsored posts (6 months) → Abandoned:** Poor ROI and operational friction

### Marketing Channels

**Primary Channel:** Word of mouth + Tech influencer endorsements

- John Gruber endorsement was pivotal
- Strategic self-linking creates discovery loops
- CEO interview access builds credibility over time

**Secondary Channels:**

- Twitter: Original discovery channel
- Podcast ecosystem: Dithering, Sharp Tech, Sharp China

**Failed Attempts:**

- Sponsored posts: Abandoned after 6 months due to poor ROI

### Revenue Model

**Pricing:** $12/month or $120/year

**Pricing Evolution:**

- Initial: $100/year ($10/month equivalent)
- Current: $120/year ($12/month)
- Stratechery Plus bundle with additional podcasts

**Conversion Rate:** Not disclosed publicly

**Key insight:** Thompson regrets raising prices and believes keeping prices accessible maximizes long-term impact.

### Challenges & Solutions

1. **Challenge:** Failed initial product launch
   - **Solution:** Pivoted to email-based delivery
   - **Result:** Discovered core product-market fit

2. **Challenge:** Scaling beyond solo operation
   - **Solution:** Added podcast co-hosts (Gruber, Bishop, Hertzfeld)
   - **Result:** Reduced churn, expanded content without hiring

## All Tactics & Strategies Used

### Tactic 1: Content Backlog Before Launch

- **What They Did:** Built multiple articles before announcing site
- **How They Implemented:** Wrote consistently while employed
- **Context:** Ensured quality and consistency from day one
- **Results Achieved:** Established credibility immediately
- **Time Investment:** Months of preparation
- **Cost:** $0 (time only)

### Tactic 2: Influencer Cold Email

- **What They Did:** Emailed John Gruber directly
- **How They Implemented:** Sent thoughtful email about analysis
- **Context:** No existing relationship, just quality content
- **Results Achieved:** Tripled Twitter followers in 12 hours
- **Time Investment:** Minutes to write email
- **Cost:** $0

### Tactic 3: Distinctive Visual Branding

- **What They Did:** Custom fonts, orange color scheme, hand-drawn illustrations
- **How They Implemented:** iPad illustrations, custom theme
- **Context:** Stand out in social media shares
- **Results Achieved:** Memorable brand recognition
- **Time Investment:** Ongoing
- **Cost:** Minimal

### Tactic 4: Strategic Self-Linking

- **What They Did:** Frequently link to prior analysis
- **How They Implemented:** Reference past articles in new pieces
- **Context:** Creates discovery loops, shows evolution of thinking
- **Results Achieved:** Increased engagement and archive value
- **Time Investment:** Integrated into writing process
- **Cost:** $0

### Tactic 5: CEO Interview Access

- **What They Did:** Transitioned from outsider to interviewing major executives
- **How They Implemented:** Built reputation over years, published full transcripts
- **Context:** Mark Zuckerberg, Jensen Huang, Satya Nadella interviews
- **Results Achieved:** Enhanced credibility, unique content
- **Time Investment:** Years of reputation building
- **Cost:** $0

### Tactic 6: Podcast Bundling

- **What They Did:** Added podcasts to subscription without raising price
- **How They Implemented:** Partnered with co-hosts (Gruber, Bishop)
- **Context:** Increase perceived value, reduce churn
- **Results Achieved:** Higher retention, expanded content offering
- **Time Investment:** Weekly podcast production
- **Cost:** Revenue sharing with co-hosts
