# Lenny's Newsletter Success Story

## The Story

### Problem Discovery

Lenny Rachitsky left Airbnb in 2019 after 7 years, planning to start a company. He wrote a Medium article about his experience that went viral (29,000 claps, shared company-wide by CEO). His friend Andrew Chen convinced him to leverage this momentum through a newsletter instead of pursuing a startup.

**Key insight:** Product managers and founders needed actionable, research-backed advice on growth, product, and building companies.

### MVP Development

**Timeline:** June 2019 (started on Medium, then Substack)

**Initial Features:**

- Free newsletter on product management topics
- Weekly publication schedule
- Research-backed insights

**Tech Stack:**

- Substack (primary platform)
- Medium (initial platform)

**Development Process:**

- Started with free content for 9 months
- Built audience before monetizing
- Focused on original research projects

### Launch Strategy

**Platform:** Medium → Substack

**Date:** June 2019 (free), April 2020 (paid)

**Pre-launch:**

- 9 months of free, valuable content
- Guest posts on Andrew Chen's platform and First Round Review
- Built to 13,000 subscribers before paid launch

**Launch Results (April 2020):**

- 486 paying subscribers in first month
- $56,000 ARR immediately
- By year end: 45,000 free, 3,300 paid subscribers
- ~$360,000 ARR by December 2020

### Growth Journey

#### Phase 1: Free Content Building (June 2019 - March 2020)

- Published weekly free content
- Guest posted on established platforms
- Reached 13,000 subscribers in 9 months
- Two major research projects doubled subscriber base

#### Phase 2: Paid Launch & Early Growth (April 2020 - 2021)

- Launched paid tier at $15/month or $150/year
- 486 paid subscribers in first month ($56K ARR)
- By year end: 3,300 paid ($360K ARR)
- Added podcast in June 2022

#### Phase 3: Exponential Growth via Recommendations (2022-Present)

- April 2022: Substack launched recommendations feature
- Subscriber base tripled: 111K → 377K in under a year
- 78% of new subscribers now come from Substack recommendations
- Current: 574,000 total, 18,000+ paid

#### Phase 4: Multi-Revenue Diversification

- Podcast brings in $500K+/year from sponsors
- Job board adds additional revenue
- Angel investing portfolio
- Estimated total: $4M+/year

### Key Decisions

1. **Newsletter over startup**
   - **Why:** Andrew Chen's advice to leverage viral Medium post
   - **Result:** Built $4M+ media business

2. **9 months free before monetizing**
   - **Why:** Build trust and audience first
   - **Result:** Strong conversion rates when launched paid

3. **Major research projects**
   - **Why:** Create 10x content that compounds
   - **Result:** "Marketplace Scaling" and "First 1,000 Users" doubled subscriber base

4. **Substack recommendations**
   - **Why:** Leverage platform network effects
   - **Result:** 78% of new subscribers from recommendations

### Pivots & Changes

- **Medium → Substack:** Moved to platform with built-in monetization
- **Newsletter only → Multi-product:** Added podcast, job board, community

### Marketing Channels

**Primary Channel:** Substack Recommendations

- 78% of new subscribers come from other newsletters
- Over 1,000 newsletters recommend Lenny's
- Tripled subscriber base in under a year

**Secondary Channels:**

- Twitter: Threads, tagging featured experts, crowdsourcing data
- Guest posts: Andrew Chen, First Round Review
- Podcast interviews

**Failed Attempts:**

- (None documented)

### Revenue Model

**Pricing:**

- Free tier: 1 post per month + previews
- Paid tier: $15/month or $150/year
- Corporate tier: $300/year
- Insider tier: $350/year (premium tools access)

**Revenue Streams:**

- Newsletter subscriptions: ~$2M/year
- Podcast sponsorships: ~$500K-$2M/year
- Job board: Growing revenue
- Angel investing: Portfolio returns

**Conversion Rate:** 4-8% (below Substack's suggested 10%)

### Challenges & Solutions

1. **Challenge:** Lower than expected conversion rate
   - **Solution:** Focus on quality over conversion optimization
   - **Result:** Still achieved $2M+ from subscriptions

2. **Challenge:** Sustainability of solo operation
   - **Solution:** Diversified into podcast and job board
   - **Result:** Multiple revenue streams, $4M+ total

## All Tactics & Strategies Used

### Tactic 1: Guest Posting on Established Platforms

- **What They Did:** Published on Medium, Andrew Chen, First Round Review
- **How They Implemented:** Leveraged existing relationships and viral content
- **Context:** Early stage before own audience
- **Results Achieved:** Reached 1,000 subscribers early
- **Time Investment:** Significant writing time
- **Cost:** $0

### Tactic 2: Epic Research Projects

- **What They Did:** Multi-month research interviewing 25+ industry leaders
- **How They Implemented:** "Marketplace Scaling" (17 companies, 25+ leaders)
- **Context:** "Create good content every week, but be working on something epic in the background"
- **Results Achieved:** "First 1,000 Users" project gained 5,000+ subscribers in two weeks
- **Time Investment:** Months per project
- **Cost:** $0 (time investment)

### Tactic 3: Twitter Flywheel

- **What They Did:** Threads promoting content, tagging experts, crowdsourcing data
- **How They Implemented:** Multiple thread angles on same content, non-aggressive approach
- **Context:** Sustainable quality following over growth hacking
- **Results Achieved:** Amplified reach through expert networks
- **Time Investment:** Regular posting
- **Cost:** $0

### Tactic 4: Substack Recommendations Leveraging

- **What They Did:** Actively participated in Substack's recommendation ecosystem
- **How They Implemented:** Both recommended others and received recommendations
- **Context:** Platform feature launched April 2022
- **Results Achieved:** Tripled subscriber base (111K → 377K in under a year)
- **Time Investment:** Ongoing relationship building
- **Cost:** $0

### Tactic 5: Full Transparency in Threads

- **What They Did:** "Give away all the best stuff from the newsletter in the thread"
- **How They Implemented:** Complete transparency drives click-through
- **Context:** Counter-intuitive approach
- **Results Achieved:** Higher engagement and trust
- **Time Investment:** Thread writing time
- **Cost:** $0
